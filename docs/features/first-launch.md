# First launch

> **Status:** draft, MVP. Last updated 2026-11-05.

What a new user sees the first time they open the app.

## What the user can do

- Read a short intro: what [spirited driving](../overview.md#spirited-driving) is, and the idea: pick a
  destination, say how much extra time or distance you'll spend, get the most fun route that fits.
- Skip the intro.
- Allow location access, with an explanation of why the map needs it.

## Rules

- It is shown once.
- It has an illustration of light sports cars on a curvy road, following the [tone](../overview.md#tone): no real
  car models.
- No sign-up: the app is ready to use right after the intro.
- The app works without location access.

## Needs and provides

- **Provides:** location permission for the [Map](map.md).

## Account

Not needed.
